# because:
b37
The Broad Institute created a human genome reference file based on GRCh37. This reference is often referred to as b37 
(Homo_sapiens_assembly19.fasta, MD5sum: 886ba1559393f75872c1cf459eb57f2d).

When people at The Broad Institute's Genomics Platform refer to the hg19 reference, they are actually referring to b37.

The humanG1Kv37 (human_g1k_v37.fasta, MD5sum: 0ce84c872fc0072a885926823dcd0338) reference is equivalent to b37, with the exception that it 
does not contain the decoy sequence for human herpesvirus 4 type 1 (named NC007605_). This reference grew out of the 1000 Genomes Project.
