## Note on MAPQ filtering in finaledb

The finaledb snakemake workflow does not apply any MAPQ filter during fragment extraction — samtools view uses -f 3 -F 3852 -G 48 --incl-flags 48 but no -q flag. All fragments are written to .frag.bed.gz with mapq in column 5. MAPQ filtering is applied entirely post-hoc in compute_features_mapq.sbatch. Differences between mapq0/15/30/45 in RBF CV1 results reflect genuinely different fragment populations, not a hidden floor in the source data.
